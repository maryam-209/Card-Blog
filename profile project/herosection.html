What is Surah Al-Ikhlas?

Surah Al-Ikhlas is the 112th chapter of the Qur’an. It is a short but powerful Surah that explains the concept of pure monotheism (Tawheed). It declares that Allah is One, Eternal, and Absolute. He has no children and was not born. Nothing is equal to Him.

Why was it revealed?

This Surah was revealed in Makkah when the Quraysh asked the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) to describe the lineage and nature of Allah. In response, Allah revealed this Surah to clearly explain His oneness and uniqueness.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Translation: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

Translation: Say, “He is Allah, the One.

ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ

Translation: Allah, the Eternal Refuge.

لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ

Translation: He neither begets nor is born.

وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

Translation: Nor is there to Him any equivalent.”

Key Lesson Box

True faith begins with understanding that Allah is One and unique. Tawheed is the foundation of Islam.
