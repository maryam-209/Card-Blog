🌙 RAMADAN KAREEM

A Month of Blessings, Mercy, and Forgiveness

🌅 Sehri (Suhoor)

Sehri is the pre-dawn meal before the fast begins. It gives strength and energy for the day ahead.

🌇 Iftar

Iftar is the meal to break the fast at sunset. It is a moment of gratitude and peace.

🤲 Dua

Ramadan is the best time to make dua. Hearts are softer, prayers are stronger, and mercy is greater..

✨Quote of the Day

“Ramadan is not just about fasting, it is about cleansing the heart, mind, and soul.”
